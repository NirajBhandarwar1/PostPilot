# Code of Conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
- Assume good faith, and say what you need clearly.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules,
and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, open a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/NirajBhandarwar1/PostPilot/security/advisories/new) and say it
is a conduct report.
